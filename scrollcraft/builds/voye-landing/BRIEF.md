# BRIEF — Voye Arts & Designs landing page (animation pass)

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The user supplied the
scroll-craft skill and the instruction "Apply some good and intentional
animation" on the existing Voye landing page. Brand facts below are evidence
from the client startup pack (`assets-inbox/Voye_Startup_Pack/`); animation

copy, brand and structure already exist and are preserved.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe (authored from brand evidence):** personalised, coastal, warm,
   personal, unhurried. References: the client's own styled photographs (gift box with a
   teal ribbon, leather book with a hand-drawn Namib map), the shell-and-rings
   badge, the Namib sunrise photo. Not sites.
2. **The journey (evidence: existing page + business profile):** arrive at a
   coastal studio counter → read the real terms → see what they make → see
   real pieces → learn the five ordering steps → receive the wrapped-piece
   promise → hear the founder's story → get practical answers → start one
   WhatsApp order.
3. **Energy (authored):** quiet the whole way, one warm swell at the
   wrapped-piece promise. Administrative parts (terms, FAQ) compressed.
4. **Feeling curve:** below. **Peak:** the ribbon.
5. **Signature move (authored):** a teal ribbon draws itself around the final
   ordering step and ties a bow on the words "beautifully wrapped". Bespoke
   SVG generated from the measured step box, drawn once on entry. Not a kit
   device; the engine is untouched.
6. **Range (evidence):** editorial, warm craft. The brand guide names cream
   paper, deep/mid teal, soft aqua, warm gold as the single accent, Lora +
   Poppins Light, sentence case. This is the brand's own palette, not the
   artisan default reach.
7. **World (evidence):** one paper ground with hard-cut teal bands (the
   stationery's own logic). Photographic: the client's own photos and
   illustrations. No generated world, no clay, no video.
8. **Assets (evidence):** everything is the client's own pack. No generation,
   no KIE key, no spend.

## Feeling curve (emotion first, cause second)

1. Hero — **recognition**: their coast and their gift occasion; prints settle
   onto paper like postcards on a counter, planes lean slightly with the pointer.
2. Facts — **reassurance**: the real terms (turnaround, student discount,
   delivery) wipe in quietly. No counters, no invented numbers.
3. Services — **clarity**: six named categories in an asymmetric spread, each
   landing once.
4. Gallery — **desire**: real pieces develop into view like prints in a
   developing tray, one after another as you scroll.
5. Process — **trust**: the ledger line draws itself down the five steps as
   you scroll; each number lands as the line reaches it.
6. Wrapped step — **delight (PEAK)**: after a quiet breath, a teal ribbon
   wraps the final step and ties a bow around "beautifully wrapped".
7. Story — **warmth**: the badge holds still while the shell, rings and sun
   lines arrive one by one; the signature line lands last.
8. FAQ — **calm**: plain answers, no motion. Administrative, compressed.
9. Enquire — **resolve**: the form fields stagger in and the page stops
   moving; one action, the WhatsApp handoff. The footer holds.

No two adjacent acts share a feeling. One peak.

windowed-reveal acts are separated by the services stagger; the two
entrance staggers are separated by the draw, cue and calm acts. Zero scrub
acts (≤2 allowed). The process act owns the largest scroll span and the
peak. Adjacent feelings all differ (curve above).

## The peak

> "A teal ribbon tied itself around my order while I was reading how it's made."

Lives in act 6 (the last step of the process). It gets the breath of empty
space before it (authored silence: the gap between step 4 and step 5 is
deliberate, not dead scroll), the largest single beat of scroll room on the
page, and the only drawn SVG event. The hero is a composition, not a
competing peak: its motion is small and slow.

## Grammar

**Studio counter** (named variant). A service business's front desk: a fixed
teal bar with one order action, a title-plus-made-objects hero, an asymmetric
catalog spread, a process ledger, a story plate, practical Q&A, and a working
order form as the close.

- **Nav:** fixed bar, wordmark + four jumps + one CTA label ("Start an order").
- **Sequence:** offer → terms → catalog → pieces → method → promise → story →
  questions → order. Hard cuts between cream and teal grounds.
- **Close:** a live form that hands over to WhatsApp, then a footer that holds.
- **Bans:** no scrub video, no pinned acts, no scroll-jacking, no continuous
  camera, no stat counters, no magnetic CTA, no scroll cues, no section
  counters, at most one eyebrow per three sections (one, on the hero).

Why not the eight: filmic one-shot and continuous world forbid the jumps and
practical FAQ a service page needs, and the brief is not travel; live surface
is for tools; typographic poster would discard the client's photography;
split stage has no comparison argument; rhythmic cutlist contradicts an
unhurried craft brand; gallery/catalog forbids the persuasion and founder
story the business profile centers; chaptered editorial forbids the fixed bar
and the media hero the client's page already uses and the user asked to keep.
The counter grammar keeps the existing structure (the user's scoped request)
and states its own bans.
