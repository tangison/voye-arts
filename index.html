How ordering works

Five steps, from first message to a beautifully wrapped piece.

  1. Enquire

    Message us on WhatsApp or through this page.

  2. Design chat and quote

    We talk through names, colours, dates and ideas, then send a clear quote.

  3. Approve and pay a 50% deposit

    We send a design preview. Once you approve it and pay the deposit, we begin.

  4. Made to your design

    Your piece is made to your approved design and quality checked before delivery.

  5. Delivery or collection

    Pay the balance and receive your piece, beautifully wrapped.

  • Students: 10% off with a valid student card
  • Urgent orders: 30% rush fee
  • Delivery charged separately

Made with purpose

Voye Arts & Designs began in 2026 with a love of making and an appreciation for custom-made things. Founder Ndamononghenda Mateus wants well-made, personalised pieces to be within reach of students, researchers, professionals, businesses and anyone with an idea.

The logo holds the story. A bivalve shell from the seabed, the home ground of a benthic ecologist. Two rings leaning together: the maker and the receiver. The Namib sun rising over the coast, and the ripple where the dunes meet the Atlantic.

Every piece holds something precious for its owner.

Good to know

How long does an order take?

Usually 5 to 10 working days, depending on the product. Tell us your date early if it is urgent.

What will it cost?

Every piece is quoted individually. You receive a clear quote after the design chat, before any payment.

How do I pay?

A 50% deposit once you approve your design preview, and the balance on delivery or collection.

Do you offer a student discount?

Yes, 10% off with a valid student card.

Can you rush an order?

Yes. Urgent orders carry a 30% rush fee, so tell us your date as early as you can.

Do you deliver?

Yes, delivery is charged separately. You can also collect in Swakopmund. We send orders across Namibia and internationally.
