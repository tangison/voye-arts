How ordering works

Five simple steps, from first message to a beautifully wrapped piece.

  1. Enquire

    Message us on WhatsApp, Instagram or through this page.

  2. Design chat and quote

    We talk through names, colours, dates and ideas, then send a clear quote.

  3. Approve and pay a 50% deposit

    We send a design preview. Once you approve it, we begin.

  4. Handmade and checked

    Your piece is made by hand and quality checked.

  5. Delivery or collection

    Pay the balance and receive your piece, beautifully wrapped.

From the studio

A look at the kind of pieces we love to make.

Made with purpose

Voye Arts & Designs began with a love of making and an appreciation for custom-made things. We want well-crafted, personalised pieces to be within reach of students, researchers, professionals, businesses and anyone with an idea.

The logo holds the story: two rings leaning together in a shell, the Namib sun rising over them, and the ripple where the dunes meet the Atlantic. Every piece holds something precious for its owner.

Good to know

How long does an order take?

Usually 5 to 10 working days, depending on the product.

How do I pay?

A 50% deposit once you approve your design preview, and the balance on delivery or collection.

Do you offer a student discount?

Yes, 10% off with a valid student card.

Can you rush an order?

Yes. Urgent orders carry a 30% rush fee, so tell us your date early.

Do you deliver?

Yes, delivery is charged separately. You can also collect in Swakopmund.
